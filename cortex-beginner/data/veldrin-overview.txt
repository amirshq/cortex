Veldrin Corp was founded in 2019 by Marta Okonkwo and Priya Raghunathan in Halifax, Nova Scotia. The company builds industrial humidity sensors for cold-chain logistics. As of the 2024 annual report, Veldrin employs 412 people across three offices: Halifax, Rotterdam, and Busan. The company's flagship product is the Kestrel-7 sensor array.
